Publikator

A personal tool for scheduling uploads of my own videos

Publikator is a small desktop application written and operated by Marcel Śmieszek. It runs on the author's own computer and uploads the author's own original videos to the author's own YouTube channels and sends the author's own short videos to the author's own TikTok inbox as drafts, at times the author chooses, using the platforms' official APIs.

What it does

  • Keeps a queue of finished videos, each with a title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Uploads each video through the official YouTube Data API at the scheduled time and applies the author's chosen settings, including the disclosure for altered or synthetic content.
  • Sends short videos to the author's own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Upload): each video arrives as a draft in the author's TikTok inbox, and the author reviews and publishes it manually in the TikTok app.
  • Shows the author a simple status page listing what was uploaded, with a link to open each video in YouTube Studio.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service. Only the author uses it, with the author's own accounts.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or other users.
  • It does not copy or re-upload other people's content.
  • It does not publish anything on TikTok by itself: every TikTok draft is published by the author in the TikTok app.
