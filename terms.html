1. About

Publikator is a personal desktop tool written and operated by Marcel Śmieszek. It is used only by its author, with accounts the author owns, to schedule uploads of the author's own original videos.

2. Use of third-party platforms

Publikator accesses YouTube through the YouTube API Services and is used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. By using Publikator, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Publikator requests only the Google permission youtube.upload and uses it only to upload the author's own videos and their thumbnails to the author's own YouTube channel. Publikator accesses TikTok through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Upload mode) and is used in accordance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Terms of Service. Videos are sent to the author's own TikTok inbox as drafts and are published by the author manually in the TikTok app.
